Who We Are

London, UK. Formed 2018. Still loud.

First Time Dead is a fast-paced, melodic punk rock band from London, UK. Their sound draws from the golden era of 90's melodic hardcore and skatepunk — Descendents, NOFX, Bad Religion, Pennywise.

Formed in 2018, they hit hard with debut album Rest in Pieces (2019), turned pandemic isolation into Nemesis of Me (2023), and are back with Turmoil (2025) — louder, faster, and further from done.

They've built a loyal following across England and Spain, played Altimira Festival twice, and shared stages with Authority Zero, Cancer Bats, MakeWar, and many more.
